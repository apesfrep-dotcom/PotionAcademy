The Potion Shop

Grade 3 liquid volume. Apprentices learn how much real containers hold, that 1 liter is 1,000 milliliters, and when to use mL or L. Then they brew potions on their own.

Teacher, projected

The Lesson

Fully narrated, so you don't need to talk. It stops at each notebook box and waits for you to press Continue (or Space).

Open the lesson Students, Chromebook

Potion Lab

Six customer orders, then four target challenges where any mix of containers works as long as it hits the exact mL or L. Finishing them unlocks the Potion Creator button, where students invent their own potion in mL and L and print a recipe card.

How to run it

  1. Print the Lab Notebook (pages 1 and 2 for students; page 3 is your answer key). Students need a pencil.
  2. Project the lesson and press Begin. Turn the volume up. Space plays, pauses, and continues.
  3. At each notebook box, the lesson stops. Press Continue when everyone has written.
  4. At the end, students open the Potion Lab on their Chromebooks.
  5. After the six orders and four target challenges, students press the Potion Creator button, invent a potion, and print a recipe card.
